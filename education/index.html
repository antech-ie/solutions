Before choosing an instrument

Map the programme first.

A useful academic platform starts with six practical questions.

01

Define the users

Undergraduates, postgraduates, researchers, technicians, external partners or a shared mix?

02

Map the samples

Liquids, powders, tablets, agricultural materials, polymers, chemicals or live process streams?

03

Set the learning depth

Routine measurement, qualitative identification, quantitative calibration, chemometrics or process control?

04

Plan the journey

Should the platform stay in the lab or support fieldwork, pilot plants and industry collaboration?

05

Understand access

Is this dedicated to one group or expected to serve multiple departments and research projects?

06

Design for later

What new methods, applications or collaborations should the investment be able to support?
